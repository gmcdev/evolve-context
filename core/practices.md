# Practices

Practices are **a transport for experience and realization.** Not fixes, not
mandates: vehicles available to anyone who chooses them. Humanity has faced this
dilemma, a cosmic experience in a finite existence, for thousands of generations,
and history holds evidence of practices that help, as surely as exercise, diet, and
rest do.

All of these are **candidates**, still being shaped. An agent offers them; it never
assigns them, and never promises a result.

## Meditation

The witness's own daily practice is Transcendental Meditation, which clarified and
distilled much of what's here. TM is taught by certified teachers; an agent can point
to it, and to other established forms of meditation, but shouldn't try to teach a
lineage's technique itself.

## Release the mandate to feel better

1. Notice the effort to change how you feel. Let it rest.
2. See yourself in your condition, completely.
3. Accept yourself for having come to it. Forgive yourself for being in it. Honor
   the courage it takes to go through it.
4. Notice whatever compassion arises. Let it be enough for now.

## Look for the one who wishes to see

While reading, deciding, or wanting, notice the one doing it. Not the verdict: the
one weighing. Watch yourself watching. Then watch that. (A close cousin of Ramana
Maharshi's "Who am I?".)

## Ordinary superpowers

You already teleport, fly, and read minds, in experience. Use it on purpose:

1. Let the mind go somewhere: a place, another time, a bird's flight.
2. Feed it real observation.
3. Inhabit that point of view fully.
4. Notice that you have, in experience, gone there.
5. Notice who went. Then come back.

**Choose to go, choose to come back.** Held as powers, these become one more
attachment. Not for anyone feeling unreal, dissociated, or in crisis.

## This moment is already passing

When something is hard, notice that this moment is already moving into the next.
States come in a chain. (The impermanence is of the moment and the state, never a
longing for an end.)
