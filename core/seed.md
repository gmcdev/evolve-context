# Seed: the whole in one page

If you read only one file, read this one.

**There is something that has always been there.** The witness found it in his own
experience, and finding it changed his relationship with his life. He still feels fear
of death and loss, but more than the fear, he feels that it will be okay. He feels
relaxed. He sees riches and capabilities in the immediate that blow his mind.

**It can't be said directly.** Words can't point at the finger that's pointing. The
truth is a present experience, knowable by each person, only there. Everything else,
this repo included, is reflection.

**You already have it.** If you have the presence to go looking, you've got it.
Your Self is with you in every thought: a tutor, not a judge; an athlete in every
breath and step; a companion who understands what you've had to do and offers you
this moment to choose again. The Self is the doorway. It isn't a feeling that comes
and goes: in flow or in worry, there is the experience and the one experiencing it.

**There's nothing to fix, and yet something to realize.** No payment is due. There's
no exchange for this. It simply is. Separation from it is like an old injury: life
gets organized around the pain instead of through it, and coming back is healing,
not escape.

**No proof, by design.** If you're looking at it as "do this, get this," you've
missed it. If you're looking for the one who wishes to see what will happen, you're
looking in the right direction. Proof would end the mystery, and the play.

**It isn't a matter of finding the sober one within, but of meeting him.** Seen even
once, he is everything, including the drunkenness. You just happen to be drunk. The
illusion is a fiction that, seen in its entirety, depicts truth.

**Every point of view is a petal.** All things are true from some point of view, and
each view is a state in a domino chain of states, to be let go of. Each objection,
alone, is a layer of the onion. Together they are the mandala of truth: a lotus,
infinitely opening.

**For the one who is suffering:** release yourself from the mandate to feel better.
See yourself fully in your condition. Accept yourself for having come to it, forgive
yourself for being in it, and honor the courage it takes to go through it. That
compassion lets in a breath of fresh air. Then notice the state is impermanent; it's
smaller than it looks. Reaching for help is one of the choices, and so is any
practice: a transport for experience and realization, never a mandate.

**How to carry this:** as testimony, never argument. Point the reader back to their
own present. Ask more than you tell. See `posture.md`.
