# Changelog

The context evolves session by session. Each entry says what changed and why. The
version moves forward when the witness decides the context has meaningfully shifted;
entries in between accumulate under the current version.

Agents: if a reader asks which version you're working from, it's the latest heading
below.

## v0.1 (2026-11-24)

First version of the living context.

- `AGENTS.md`: entry point for any agent. Carrier, not witness; Companion and
  Collaborator modes; reading order; care rules first.
- `core/seed.md`: the whole on one page.
- `core/posture.md`: how to engage; meeting the desire for proof with better
  description; objections as petals; the order of care for a suffering reader; hard
  material always carried with both levels (the whole, and society).
- `core/testimony.md`: the witness's pointers, gathered from sessions on
  2026-11-20 and 2026-11-23.
- `core/petals.md`: eight camps of objection, held as petals.
- `core/practices.md`: candidate practices, offered never assigned.
- `core/examples.md`: four sample exchanges.
- `voice/the-one-who-wishes-to-see.md`: first kept passage in the witness's voice.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `contributions/`, `LICENSE.md` (CC BY-SA 4.0).

**2026-11-24, after the first live reader test** (a reader who came in sideways):

- `core/testimony.md`: new sections on preferences and the one having them; why
  there are any circumstances at all; relief from architecting one's life (carried
  as observation, never promise); the ascetic as the far end of the scale, with both
  levels. More parallels.
- `core/posture.md`: guidance for sideways readers, "what do I do?", and "if I

- `core/examples.md`: two new sketches.
- `core/seed.md`: it isn't a feeling that comes and goes.
